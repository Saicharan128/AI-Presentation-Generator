Understanding Climate Change's Global Impact

  • Earth's average temperature has increased by approximately 1.1°C since pre-industrial times due to greenhouse gas emissions from human activities.
  • Rising sea levels, caused by melting polar ice caps and thermal expansion of oceans, threaten coastal communities and island nations worldwide.
  • Extreme weather events, including more frequent hurricanes, droughts, and floods, are becoming increasingly common as atmospheric patterns shift.
  • The burning of fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes continue to release carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases at unprecedented rates.
  • Scientists project that without significant emission reductions, global temperatures could rise by up to 4°C by 2100, leading to catastrophic environmental changes.
